# Products

This directory is reserved for product workspaces that the Founder authorizes after Gate 5 validation and Gate 6 product-definition review. Foundation v0.1 selects no product and creates no application.

Each workspace must link its stable opportunity ID, investment decision, validation result, approved PRD, architecture decisions, engineering issues, risk register, and governance reviews. A directory name or draft PRD does not itself authorize implementation.

Product work must remain isolated by workspace, preserve out-of-scope boundaries, and use reviewed GitHub issues for Engineering execution. If Gate 6 has not passed, keep the proposed definition in the authorized research or decision workflow rather than creating a product workspace.

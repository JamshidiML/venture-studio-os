# Governance

This directory stores independent reviews for stage gates, investment artifacts, validation, product definition, engineering readiness, and release. Governance evaluates the submitted version and does not silently rewrite its source.

Use [`templates/GOVERNANCE_REVIEW.md`](../templates/GOVERNANCE_REVIEW.md) and identify critical blockers separately from recommendations. Reviews preserve independence conflicts, evidence gaps, scoring rationale, required corrections, conditions, and exactly one decision: `APPROVE`, `APPROVE WITH CONDITIONS`, `REWORK REQUIRED`, or `REJECT`.

A Governance decision informs the Founder but does not merge work, accept legal or financial risk, or advance a gate by itself. Store reviews under an ISO-dated gate or scope path and link the exact artifact version reviewed.

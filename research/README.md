# Research

This directory stores authorized market discovery, screening, due diligence, and validation evidence. It is currently a structural guide; Foundation v0.1 contains no market findings or product candidates.

## Allowed content

- scoped methodology and source registers
- opportunity artifacts identified by `OPP-YYYY-NNN`
- permitted synthesized evidence and validation results
- explicit limitations, consent conditions, and provenance

## Rules

Research begins only from a Founder-approved issue naming the gate and scope. Use the matching template, lifecycle front matter, ISO dates, and the claim taxonomy in [`docs/EVIDENCE_AND_CONFIDENCE_RULES.md`](../docs/EVIDENCE_AND_CONFIDENCE_RULES.md). Do not store credentials, unnecessary personal data, raw private recordings, or licensed source copies.

Suggested paths are `research/[scope]/YYYY-MM-DD-market-screening.md` and `research/OPP-YYYY-NNN/`. Draft research cannot authorize product selection or implementation.

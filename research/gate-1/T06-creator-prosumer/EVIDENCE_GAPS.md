## Evidence ceiling

The opportunity universe is complete for Gate 1, but commercial demand, WTP, retention, global applicability, store acceptance, API access, composite-preflight distinctness and rights accuracy remain unproven. Direct evidence strengthens multi-stage/accessibility context only. Creator quality can be complete without pretending those opportunity uncertainties are resolved.

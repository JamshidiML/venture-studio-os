## Evidence ceiling

The opportunity universe is complete for Gate 1, but commercial demand, WTP, retention, global applicability, store acceptance, API access and rights accuracy remain unproven. Creator quality can be complete without pretending those opportunity uncertainties are resolved. Governance review remains pending.

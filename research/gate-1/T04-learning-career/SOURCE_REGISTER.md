## Source-selection rule

Primary statistical agencies, intergovernmental datasets, regulators and official occupational resources were preferred. Vendor marketing pages, unsourced market-size pages, app-store rankings, social posts and estimated download/revenue sites were excluded from material claims. The register supports a broad opportunity universe; it does not prove product-market fit, willingness to pay or learning efficacy for any candidate.

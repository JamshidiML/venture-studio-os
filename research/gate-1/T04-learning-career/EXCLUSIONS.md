# T04 Exclusions

| Excluded space | Reason | Evidence or rule applied |
|---|---|---|
| Accredited degree or certificate platform | Outside micro-tool scope; accreditation and content operations are structurally different | Issue #7 hard boundary |
| School administration, LMS or classroom surveillance | Enterprise/institutional workflow and heightened student privacy | Issue #7; L10, L12 |
| Essay, exam or assignment answer generator | Enables academic misconduct and weakens educational integrity | Issue #7; L10 |
| Automated applicant ranking, resume rejection or hiring recommendation | High-stakes employment decision with discrimination risk | Issue #7; L11 |
| Synthetic resume experience or credential generator | Deceptive and unsafe | Issue #7; evidence rules |
| Scraped commercial course summarizer | Copyright and platform-term exposure | Issue #7 content-rights boundary |
| Health, clinical or safety certification training | Explicitly outside scope and may create harmful reliance | Issue #7 hard boundary |
| Enterprise HR suite or employee-monitoring product | Different buyer, authority and privacy model | Issue #7 hard boundary |
| Generic to-do list, calendar or browser utility | Belongs to another thread unless learning/career workflow is essential | Parent #3 thread isolation |
| Child-directed autonomous AI tutor | Age, privacy, accuracy and pedagogical-validation risk exceeds this bounded adult-first universe | L10, L12 |
| Accent scoring for employability | Risks discrimination and harmful normative judgments | L11; accessibility and fairness rule |
| Guaranteed-job or guaranteed-learning-outcome product | Unsupported outcome and deceptive marketing risk | Evidence rules; L05 is contextual only |

Rejected ideas remain excluded; they were not silently converted into candidates.

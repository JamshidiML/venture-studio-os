## Evidence ceiling statement

There is no access blocker to public-source Gate 1 work. The ceiling applies to **candidate confidence**, not artifact quality: direct demand, willingness to pay, retention, and compliance feasibility require separately authorized primary research. The artifact can still reach 100/100 execution quality by representing those gaps without inflation.

## Selection rule

Current public-health agencies, regulators and official platform policies were prioritized. Disease-treatment studies, supplement marketing, app-store rankings, testimonials, opaque market-size estimates and unverified health claims were excluded. The retained evidence establishes recurring wellness contexts and hard safety constraints; it does not establish clinical benefit, WTP or candidate-level demand.

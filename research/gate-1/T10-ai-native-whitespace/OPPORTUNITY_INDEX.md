These contracts describe later tests only; none was executed in Gate 1.

Planned review, only after separate authorization: within two weeks, audit the named directories/associations and complete five channel-owner conversations. A candidate fails this reachability assumption if no compliant contact path or recruiting partner is found.
- **D-contract — defensibility hypothesis:** measure whether the candidate-specific, user-approved asset named below reduces median configuration plus review time on a second comparable corpus versus a generic workflow while still meeting the candidate's quality contract. Success: at least 30% reduction for at least three of five independently recruited target teams. Kill: less than 10% median reduction or no lawful reusable asset for at least four of five teams. Time box: six weeks after separate validation authorization.

### Overlap boundary decisions

- `900` maps claims inside a policy-analysis corpus; `904` adjudicates contradictions and boundary conditions across research papers. Different users, outputs, and error decisions keep them separate.
- `901` transcribes page content with uncertainty; `917` reconciles museum object metadata, images, labels, and accession records. Text creation and collection-data quality are not combined.
- `903` extracts scientific numeric evidence; `914` drafts a procedure from a technical diagram under engineer authority; `916` flags contradictions across requirements/drawings/tests. Their failure costs and evaluation units differ.
- `907` maps consultation themes/minority views; `908` constructs a source-linked event chronology; `918` suggests sensitive spans for an authorized records officer. No candidate shares the other’s output or release authority.

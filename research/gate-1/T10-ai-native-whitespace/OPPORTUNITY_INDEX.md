# T10 Opportunity Index

These 20 candidates are unranked Gate 1 inferences. They are not validated demand, model benchmarks, or build authorization. The two tables together provide every candidate field required by Issue #13.

## Shared future-test contracts

These contracts describe later tests only; none was executed in Gate 1.

- **R-contract — reachability assumption:** Strategy Agent owns each named channel assumption. If wrong, the candidate may have no economical acquisition path. Planned review, only after separate authorization: within two weeks, audit the named directories/associations and complete five channel-owner conversations. A candidate fails this reachability assumption if no compliant contact path or recruiting partner is found.
- **D-contract — defensibility hypothesis:** measure whether the candidate-specific, user-approved asset named below reduces median configuration plus review time on a second comparable corpus versus a generic workflow while still meeting the candidate's quality contract. Success: at least 30% reduction for at least three of five independently recruited target teams. Kill: less than 10% median reduction or no lawful reusable asset for at least four of five teams. Time box: six weeks after separate validation authorization.

# T07 Search Log

## Research protocol

- Run date and access date: 2026-07-22.
- Source order: government/regulator or standards body; first-party product documentation; secondary evidence only if needed.
- Geography tags: U.S., UK, EU, or vendor-defined/global.
- Selection test: source had to support a named material claim, constraint, workflow category, or paid adjacent category.
- Rejection test: undated summaries without provenance, SEO listicles, fabricated precision, unsourced market-size pages, and content outside Issue #10.

## Reproducibility notes

The source register preserves clean canonical URLs and access dates. Search-result snippets were used only to locate the underlying source. Material facts were recorded only when visible on the source page/result and are quoted minimally or paraphrased. “Date unavailable” was used rather than inferring a day. A future reviewer should re-open dynamic vendor, review-aggregator, regulatory, and guidance pages before relying on them.
